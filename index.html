Star schema in BigQueryBuilt with dbt

        

          
        

        

          
Why Spark instead of pandas?
A month is millions of rows. Spark processes it in parallel and writes Parquet directly, so the job never has to fit a whole month in one Python process.

          
Why idempotent monthly loads?
The loader deletes the month before appending it. Re-running a failed or corrected month never duplicates data.

          
Why an incremental, partitioned fact table?
dbt merges only the latest month instead of rebuilding history, and monthly partitions with clustering reduce BigQuery scan costs.

          
Why dbt tests inside the DAG?
A failing test fails the Airflow task, so bad data stops before it reaches the dashboard.
